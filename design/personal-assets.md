# Personal cast artwork

Created with built-in ImageGen for the website. No ChatGPT Pet records were created or changed.

- `gon.webp` is the black-and-white tuxedo cat with a white blaze, bib and paws. References: Arthur's supplied `IMG_6364.jpeg` and `IMG_3610(1).jpeg`; the previous 2D companion drawing guided style only.
- `pitou.webp` is the gray-and-white cat with a white bib and paws and a fluffy gray tail. Same photograph references and 2D style guide.
- `killua-memory.webp` is Arthur gently petting tri-color Killua, based on the original `public/assets/blog/grief/cover.png`. Arthur's expression was revised to a quiet closed-mouth almost-smile. The post's original wording and photograph remain available.
- `killua-chase.webp` is a small fictional vignette of the tri-color kitten running with Killua Zoldyck from Hunter × Hunter. It appears after the full essay so it does not interrupt reading. The artwork is a fan reference, not official franchise art.

Generation involved photo-grounded 2D illustration and a second edit to bring Gon and Pitou into the website's compact outlined companion style. All public files are transparent optimized WebP. Their full-resolution generated PNGs are local intermediates under `design/source/`.
