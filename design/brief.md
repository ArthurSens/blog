Email: free only, Brevo hosted signup links per language, double opt-in configured in Brevo. No custom database. Draft first; every delivery requires Arthur's review and approval. No automatic-send code. Until configured, the site offers working language-specific RSS feeds rather than a nonfunctional signup form.

Implementation: retain Next.js because existing static generation, Vercel deployment, locale routes and Markdown publishing already serve the brief. No article URL, translation relationship, or original post is changed. Future interactive explanations should be deliberate, story-specific components; no automatic scene generation or CMS forms.

Growth: keep Vercel's aggregate analytics and use Brevo's confirmed subscriber counts when configured. Vercel's short-lived visitor identifiers do not establish reliable cross-week returning readership; this metric is explicitly unresolved under the current free/minimal-data setup.

Review scope: this is a draft redesign for visual review, not a production merge. Exact palette, rendering, and choreography remain open to refinement.
