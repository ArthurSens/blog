## Selected files

- `../public/assets/cast/prometheus.png`: orange flame/torch companion, 1205 × 1305 RGBA PNG.
- `../public/assets/cast/opentelemetry.png`: blue/gold telescope companion, 1205 × 1305 RGBA PNG.

The tool-generated PNGs were copied directly without visual modification. Both contain genuine transparent alpha (0–255). Visual inspection confirmed complete characters, recognizable brand colors and shapes, dark outlines, no text, and no scenery. They are single poses suitable for modest whole-character CSS reactions; they are not frame-by-frame animation atlases. Shading is slightly softer than the initial flat-color request.
