The connected homepage cast scene is documented in `family-rig.md`; the superseded pose sheet is documented in `family-scene.md`.

Full generation prompts and visual inspection notes are in `brand-assets.md`, `personal-assets.md`, and `exercise-assets.md`. All new illustrations were created using built-in ImageGen. No programmatic stand-in artwork was drawn.

The public website consumes optimized `.webp` assets: cats and project characters at up to 320px; memorial at up to 1200×720; training and waving sprite strips encoded losslessly. Optimized website assets are committed. Full-resolution PNG masters and reference downloads in `design/source` are local generation intermediates; the original Arth remains in the Pets collection.
